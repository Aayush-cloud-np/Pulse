About Pulse

Built for real progress

Pulse helps you plan a fitness direction based on your own goals, record every workout and measurement, and see your progress laid out clearly over time — so consistency is something you can actually see, not just feel.

A personalized training direction based on your goal, experience, and equipment
Simple workout logging with dates, duration, sets, and notes
Visual charts and summaries that turn your history into progress
Mission
Consistency over intensity
Why Pulse

Every tool you need, refined

Four essentials that turn effort into evidence, without the clutter of a hundred half-used features.

Visual Progress Tracking

Compare workout history, weight, and photos in one clear, evolving view.

Personalized Direction

A training direction built from your goal, experience, equipment, and available days — not a generic template.

Workout Logging

Log sessions in seconds and browse a small library of workout ideas by category.

Goal & Nutrition Estimates

Weekly workout goals plus general, transparent nutrition estimates — clearly labeled, never prescriptive.
